VERDICT Study

Estimated time: 10 minutes

Radiology residents and radiologists worldwide.

This study examines how radiologists reach diagnostic and reporting decisions across clinical settings. You will read three short hypothetical scenarios and indicate how you would report each. There are no right or wrong answers.

Participation is voluntary, fully anonymous, and can be withdrawn at any time. No identifying information is collected, and the data is used solely for statistical and academic purposes.
