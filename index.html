Study Participation

AI-Assisted Intracranial Aneurysm Detection
Estimated Time: 60 minutes

  • Readers with at least 6 months of experience in reading brain MRI scans.
  • Open to residents, general radiologists, and neuroradiologists.
  • No prior exposure to AI tools necessary.

Conducted at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), this study evaluates how AI-supported diagnosis affects radiologists assessing brain MRIs for possible aneurysms.

  • Review 20 brain MRI cases flagged by an AI system.
  • Provide a diagnosis and suggest follow-up recommendations.
  • Participate online anytime via desktop or laptop.
  • Fully Anonymous: No names, emails, or identifying personal data are collected.
  • Data is strictly used for statistical analysis and academic publication.
  • Participation is entirely voluntary; you may exit the study at any time.
  • Approved by the TUM Ethics Committee.
